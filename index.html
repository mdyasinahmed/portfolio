A Hybrid Geospatial-Machine Learning Framework for Equitable Healthcare Resource Realignment in Bangladesh

Investigating the structural imbalance in healthcare resource distribution across Bangladesh's Upazila Health Complexes (UHCs). Rather than advocating for economically infeasible new construction, this research develops a data-driven Decision Support System (DSS) to optimize existing beds, specialists, and clinical capacity.

  • Enhanced 2SFCA Modeling: Calculating travel-time accessibility thresholds with distance-decay functions via OpenStreetMap road networks.
  • Clinical Pressure vs. Capacity: Synthesizing Bed Occupancy Rates (BOR) with latest demographic census & poverty indices to expose true catchment stress.
  • Unsupervised K-Means Optimization: Segmenting 490+ upazilas into actionable policy clusters to guide algorithmic resource realignment without political bias.
